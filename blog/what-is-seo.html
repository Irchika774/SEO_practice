What is SEO?

By Waru • September 1, 2026 • 6 min read

Introduction

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It is the practice of improving a website so that it ranks higher in search engine results pages (SERPs) like Google, Bing, or DuckDuckGo — and therefore receives more organic (unpaid) traffic.

Think of SEO as making your website more understandable and trustworthy, both for search engines and for real humans.

How Do Search Engines Work?

Search engines follow a three-step process to deliver results:

  1. Crawling – Bots (called "spiders" or "crawlers") continuously browse the web, following links from page to page.
  2. Indexing – Pages discovered during crawling are added to a giant database called the index.
  3. Ranking – When a user searches, the engine retrieves indexed pages and ranks them by relevance and authority.

The Three Pillars of SEO

1. On-Page SEO

This involves everything you control on your page: titles, meta descriptions, headings (H1–H6), image alt text, content quality, and internal links.

2. Off-Page SEO

This covers signals that come from outside your website — primarily backlinks (other sites linking to yours). More high-quality backlinks = higher authority.

3. Technical SEO

This ensures your site is fast, mobile-friendly, secure (HTTPS), and crawlable. It includes things like sitemaps, robots.txt, and structured data.

What Are Ranking Factors?

Google uses over 200 ranking signals. The most important ones include:

  • Content quality and relevance
  • Backlinks (number and quality)
  • Page load speed
  • Mobile-friendliness
  • User experience signals (bounce rate, dwell time)
  • Secure protocol (HTTPS)
  • Keyword usage

What is a SERP?

A SERP (Search Engine Results Page) is the page you see after typing a query into Google. It typically contains:

  • Organic results (ranked pages)
  • Paid ads (Google Ads)
  • Featured snippets (the "position zero" box)
  • Knowledge panels, People Also Ask, local packs, and more

Why Does SEO Matter?

Unlike paid advertising, organic traffic is free and sustainable. Once a page ranks, it can drive traffic for months or years without ongoing spending. This makes SEO one of the highest-ROI marketing channels available.

Summary

SEO is the art and science of helping search engines discover, understand, and rank your content. It combines technical work, high-quality writing, and relationship building (link acquisition). The good news? You can start improving your SEO today with just a few foundational steps.
