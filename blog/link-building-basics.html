Link Building Basics

By Waru • September 15, 2026 • 8 min read

What is Link Building?

Link building is the process of acquiring hyperlinks from other websites that point back to your own. These incoming links are called backlinks (or inbound links).

Backlinks act like votes of confidence. When a reputable site links to your content, it signals to Google that your page is trustworthy and authoritative — which boosts your rankings.

Why Do Backlinks Matter?

  • Authority: Links pass "link equity" (sometimes called PageRank) to your site.
  • Crawlability: New pages are often discovered by Googlebot through links.
  • Referral traffic: Users click links on other sites and land on yours.
  • Trust signals: Links from established, topically relevant sites boost your E-E-A-T.

Not All Links Are Equal

Domain Authority (DA) / Domain Rating (DR)

These are third-party metrics that estimate how authoritative a website is on a 0–100 scale. A link from a high-authority news site is worth far more than many links from low-authority blogs.

Topical Relevance

A backlink from a running blog to your "best running shoes" article is more powerful than a link from a cooking website.

Anchor Text

Anchor text is the clickable text of a hyperlink.

  • Exact-match anchors (e.g., "running shoes") can be powerful but over-use triggers Google penalties.
  • Branded anchors (e.g., "Waru SEO Practice") are the safest.
  • Natural anchors (e.g., "click here", "this article") look organic.

DoFollow vs. NoFollow

  • rel="dofollow" – passes link equity (what you want).
  • rel="nofollow" – tells Google not to count the link for ranking.

White Hat Link Building Strategies

1. Create Link-Worthy Content

The best backlinks are earned naturally. Publish content so useful that people want to reference it:

  • Original research and statistics
  • Comprehensive "ultimate guides"
  • Free tools, templates, or calculators
  • Compelling infographics

2. Guest Posting

Write a high-quality article for another blog in your niche. In return, you typically get an author bio link or an in-content link back to your site.

3. Broken Link Building

Find pages in your niche that have broken outbound links. Reach out to the site owner, notify them of the dead link, and suggest your relevant content as a replacement.

4. Skyscraper Technique

  1. Find popular content in your niche with lots of backlinks.
  2. Create a significantly better, more comprehensive version.
  3. Reach out to sites linking to the original and suggest your improved resource.

5. Digital PR

Get quoted in news articles, industry roundups, or podcasts. HARO (Help a Reporter Out) is a free service that connects journalists with expert sources.

What to Avoid

  • ❌ Buying backlinks
  • ❌ Link farms and private blog networks (PBNs)
  • ❌ Excessive reciprocal links
  • ❌ Spammy blog comments with keyword-stuffed anchor text

Tracking Your Backlinks

  • Google Search Console → Links report (free)
  • Ahrefs – the gold standard for backlink analysis
  • Moz Link Explorer – free tier available
  • Semrush – comprehensive SEO suite

Summary

Link building is a long-term strategy that rewards patience and quality. Focus on creating genuinely valuable content, build relationships in your niche, and let links come naturally. Avoid shortcuts — Google's algorithms are increasingly good at detecting manipulative tactics.
